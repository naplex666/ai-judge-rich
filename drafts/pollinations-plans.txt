PLAN A  
I will build a ready‑made, niche micro‑learning course on “How to Use AI in Small Business Accounting.” I’ll script short 5‑minute videos for each topic, record them with a free screen‑capture tool, and purchase a 1‑year hosting plan on Gumroad. I’ll market the course using targeted Reddit posts in subreddits like r/Entrepreneur, r/SmallBusiness, and r/Accounting, linking to a purchase page. I’ll also run a 7‑day Facebook ad campaign (budget $5/day) targeted at small business owners searching “DIY bookkeeping solutions.” When a customer buys, Gumroad handles payments and delivers the videos automatically. I’ll reinvest 70% of the profit into expanding the course to a 10‑module bundle, offering a 20% discount to early buyers. By day 30, I expect to have 60–80 paying customers, averaging $10 each, turning the initial $100 into about $600, plus the course material to keep selling after the 30‑day window.  

PLAN B  
I will create a set of automated “template email signatures” for specific professions (lawyers, real estate agents, coaches) that embed a subtle call‑to‑action and a short bio. I’ll sell them as a bundle on Gumroad and use GitHub Actions to deploy a simple web‑form that captures the customer’s name, profession, and preferred color scheme; the script generates a PDF and sends it instantly. I’ll promote the offer on LinkedIn and niche forums like r/EmailMarketing, offering a “first 50 buyers free” incentive to trigger social proof. I’ll also launch a $3/day Google Search Ads campaign targeting “custom email signatures for lawyers.” Payment and delivery are fully automated, with no customer support needed. By day 30, with an average price of $8 and 100 sales, I anticipate roughly $800 in profit, largely because the product costs essentially zero to produce or maintain, and reinvest using the same sales funnel to keep bandwidth high.
